Crafting Mobile Experiences That Matter

I'm a Senior Android Developer / Team Leader at Tagway (Dubai, UAE), working remotely from Mumbai. Since 2018 I've owned the Tagway RFID platform's mobile apps end-to-end: architecture, releases and production reliability for apps running on handheld scanners in warehouses, laundries and hotels, where a wrong count or a crash costs a customer real money.
